Xpences privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Xpences is an expense tracker with an AI assistant. This page explains what it collects, where that goes, and how you can delete it.

The most important things to know

  • Your money records are stored so that only you can read them. The app's admin can't see them.
  • Using the AI assistant sends what you give it, along with a list of your recent expenses, to an AI service: Groq, Google, Cloudflare or OpenRouter. Which one answers depends on which is free at that moment.
  • These are free services. Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and Google's human reviewers may read it. Some free services reached through OpenRouter may keep it and use it to train their AI. Please don't send anything you wouldn't want read.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools. Error and crash reports are used only to fix problems.
  • Xpences is for people aged 18 or over.

What we don't keep

  • The sound of voice messages and live chats is sent to the AI and isn't saved by the app. A written version of what you said is saved in the chat, after the checks described below.
  • Photos, PDFs and spreadsheets are sent to the AI and aren't saved by the app, unless you choose to keep a photo or PDF as a receipt. Only the file's name stays in your chat.

"Aren't saved by the app" describes Xpences only. See the next section for what the AI services may do with them.

What goes to the AI services

Nothing goes to the AI until you send a message, voice message or file, or start a live chat. When you do, the following is sent through our server to an AI service:

  • What you sent: your message, voice recording, live chat sound, photo, PDF or spreadsheet
  • Your last 100 expenses (date, title, category and amount), your currency and your monthly budget
  • Up to your last 10 messages in that chat from the past 10 days, and the names of your categories

Which service gets it:

  • Typed messages go to Groq first. When Groq's free allowance is used up or it is busy, they go to Google's Gemini, then to Cloudflare's own AI, and last to OpenRouter.
  • Voice messages are first written out by Groq, or by Cloudflare if Groq can't. The written words get the checks described below, then go on like a typed message. If neither can write them out, the recording goes to Google's Gemini instead.
  • Photos, PDFs, spreadsheets and live chats go to Google's Gemini only.

Groq says it doesn't use what is sent to train AI, and we have turned on its setting not to keep it. See Groq's privacy policy. Cloudflare already runs our server, and says it doesn't use what is sent to its AI to train AI. OpenRouter passes what is sent to another company's free AI. OpenRouter says some of these free services may keep it and use it to train their AI. See OpenRouter's privacy policy.

Google's terms for its free service say that it may use this content and the AI's replies to provide, improve and develop its products. They also say human reviewers may read it, after Google disconnects it from our Google account and API key. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy and the Gemini API terms. Once something has been sent, we can't delete it from any of these services.

How we protect sensitive details

  • Typed messages and spreadsheets holding an OTP, PIN, password, NID number or recovery phrase are blocked and aren't sent.
  • Account, card and phone numbers in typed messages and spreadsheets are hidden before they are sent or saved.
  • Written versions of live chats and voice messages get the same checks before they are shown or saved. A voice message written out by Groq or Cloudflare is checked before it goes on to the AI, and one holding a code is deleted and not sent.
  • Numbers inside photos and PDFs, such as a card number on a receipt, can't be hidden automatically, so avoid photos that show a full card number.

These checks can't cover sound or pictures. Voice recordings, live chat sound, photos and PDFs reach the AI exactly as they are, including anything stored inside the file. Please don't say or show codes, passwords or full account numbers, and cover them on a receipt or statement before you photograph it.

Your phone's permissions and SMS

  • Microphone: used only after you tap the voice message or live chat button.
  • Notifications: used only for the reminders you keep on in Profile. They are planned on your phone, and nothing about them is sent to us.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: only if you turn it on for the PIN lock. Your phone checks it, and Xpences never sees or stores your fingerprint or face.
  • Home screen widget: shows today's and this month's spending on your home screen. You can hide the amounts in Profile.
  • Camera and files: used only when you choose Camera, Gallery or Files, and only for the item you pick.
  • SMS: Xpences never reads your SMS inbox. It only sees a message you paste or share into it yourself. Wallet and bank messages it recognises are read on your phone and aren't sent to the AI. Other messages you paste are treated like any typed message.

Google Drive and Google Sheets

You can link Google Drive and Google Sheets in Profile, under Connected apps. This is optional, and only the Google account you signed in with can be linked.

  • What Xpences can reach: only the files it creates in your Drive (your Xpences backups and exported sheets). It can't see, open or change any of your other files.
  • What is sent: a backup holds all your money records and chats, the same as a downloaded backup file. An export holds the records you chose to export. They go straight from the app to your Google account and are stored there, under Google's terms.
  • Automatic backups run only while the app is open, at the time you choose, and you can turn them off.
  • Receipts in backups: only if you turn on Include receipts in Drive backup (it is off unless you do). Each receipt is added once, to a Receipts folder in your Xpences backups folder. If you back up with a password, the receipts are locked with it too. Receipts you delete in the app stay in your Drive until you delete them there.
  • Add to Google Calendar needs no linking. It opens Google Calendar with the event filled in (for example the loan name, amount and date), and nothing is saved there until you tap Save.
  • Unlinking both apps removes our permission key and asks Google to cancel the permission. Backups and sheets already in your Drive stay there until you delete them. You can also remove access in your Google account settings.

Who handles your data

  • Groq: the first AI service for typed messages, and writing out voice messages
  • Google: sign-in, database and app hosting (Firebase), the AI (Gemini), crash reports from the phone app (Firebase Crashlytics), which hold the error, the phone model and Android version and are not linked to your name or username, and, if you link them, Google Drive and Google Sheets
  • Cloudflare: runs our server, which passes your AI requests to the AI services and keeps the daily counts. Its own AI is a backup AI service and writes out voice messages when Groq can't. It also stores the receipts you choose to keep. It doesn't store your messages, recordings or other files. Our server's technical logs may include error details.
  • OpenRouter: the last backup AI service, which passes typed messages to another company's free AI

The app also downloads currency exchange rates from open.er-api.com. That request is made by our server and contains nothing about you.

What the admin can see

The app's admin can see login details only: your email address, username, when you joined and last signed in, whether your account is suspended, and your devices and device requests. The admin can approve or remove devices, suspend an account, or delete an account. The admin also reads the comments you send in Send feedback, shown with your username and email address, and error reports, shown with your username. The admin has no access to your expenses, accounts, loans, chats or settings.

Each admin action is recorded in a log with your user ID, your username, the action and the time.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. The one exception is chat history, which keeps 20 chats. When it is full, starting a new chat permanently removes your oldest unpinned chat, and tells you when it does. Pinned chats are never removed this way. If all 20 are pinned, new chats aren't saved until you make room.

  • To delete everything: open Profile, choose Delete my account, and confirm with Google. This permanently removes your account, money records, chats, receipts, devices, daily counts, error reports and any Google link from our systems. Backups and sheets in your own Google Drive are yours, and stay there until you delete them.
  • To delete one chat: open the chat list and tap the delete button beside it.
  • To delete a receipt: open Receipts (in the chat list or on Transactions), open the receipt and tap Delete, or select several and delete them together.
  • To ask for help: write to the address below.

Deleting doesn't remove anything already sent to the AI services, as explained above. It also doesn't remove entries in the admin action log, which stay as a record of what the admin did.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Database rules limit your records to your own account on an approved device. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise that nothing will ever go wrong.

Age and regions

You must be 18 or over to use Xpences, as Google's terms require for apps built on this service. Xpences isn't offered in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom.
