XpenceX privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

XpenceX is an expense tracker with an AI assistant. This page explains what it collects, where that goes, and how you can delete it.

The most important things to know

  • Your money records are stored so that only you can read them. The app's admin can't see them.
  • Using the AI assistant sends what you give it to Google, along with a list of your recent expenses.
  • The assistant runs on Google's free Gemini service. Google may use what is sent to improve its products, and Google's human reviewers may read it. Please don't send anything you wouldn't want read.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • XpenceX is for people aged 18 or over.

What we don't keep

  • The sound of voice messages and live chats is sent to the AI and isn't saved by the app. A written version of what you said is saved in the chat, after the checks described below.
  • Photos, PDFs and spreadsheets are sent to the AI and aren't saved by the app. Only the file's name stays in your chat.

"Aren't saved by the app" describes XpenceX only. See the next section for what Google may do with them.

What goes to Google's AI

Nothing goes to the AI until you send a message, voice message or file, or start a live chat. When you do, the following is sent through our server to Google's Gemini service:

  • What you sent: your message, voice recording, live chat sound, photo, PDF or spreadsheet
  • Your last 100 expenses (date, title, category and amount), your currency and your monthly budget
  • Up to your last 10 messages in that chat from the past 10 days, and the names of your categories

Google's terms for its free service say that it may use this content and the AI's replies to provide, improve and develop its products. They also say human reviewers may read it, after Google disconnects it from our Google account and API key. Google's use of this data is covered by Google's privacy policy and the Gemini API terms. Once something has been sent, we can't delete it from Google.

How we protect sensitive details

  • Typed messages and spreadsheets holding an OTP, PIN, password, NID number or recovery phrase are blocked and aren't sent.
  • Account, card and phone numbers in typed messages and spreadsheets are hidden before they are sent or saved.
  • Written versions of live chats and voice messages get the same checks before they are shown or saved.

These checks can't cover sound or pictures. Voice recordings, live chat sound, photos and PDFs reach the AI exactly as they are, including anything stored inside the file. Please don't say or show codes, passwords or full account numbers, and cover them on a receipt or statement before you photograph it.

Your phone's permissions and SMS

  • Microphone: used only after you tap the voice message or live chat button.
  • Camera and files: used only when you choose Camera, Gallery or Files, and only for the item you pick.
  • SMS: XpenceX never reads your SMS inbox. It only sees a message you paste or share into it yourself. Wallet and bank messages it recognises are read on your phone and aren't sent to the AI. Other messages you paste are treated like any typed message.

Who handles your data

  • Google: sign-in, database and app hosting (Firebase), and the AI (Gemini)
  • Cloudflare: runs our server, which passes your AI requests to Google and keeps the daily counts. It doesn't store your messages, recordings or files. Our server's technical logs may include error details.

The app also downloads currency exchange rates from open.er-api.com. That request is made by our server and contains nothing about you.

What the admin can see

The app's admin can see login details only: your email address, username, when you joined and last signed in, whether your account is suspended, and your devices and device requests. The admin can approve or remove devices, suspend an account, or delete an account. The admin has no access to your expenses, accounts, loans, chats or settings.

Each admin action is recorded in a log with your user ID, your username, the action and the time.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data until you delete it or delete your account. The one exception is chat history, which keeps 20 chats. When it is full, starting a new chat permanently removes your oldest unpinned chat, and tells you when it does. Pinned chats are never removed this way. If all 20 are pinned, new chats aren't saved until you make room.

  • To delete everything: open Profile, choose Delete my account, and confirm with Google. This permanently removes your account, money records, chats, devices and daily counts from our systems.
  • To delete one chat: open the chat list and tap the delete button beside it.
  • To ask for help: write to the address below.

Deleting doesn't remove anything already sent to Google's AI, as explained above. It also doesn't remove entries in the admin action log, which stay as a record of what the admin did.

Security

Data travels over encrypted connections. Database rules limit your records to your own account on an approved device. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't promise that nothing will ever go wrong.

Age and regions

You must be 18 or over to use XpenceX, as Google's terms require for apps built on this service. XpenceX isn't offered in the European Economic Area, Switzerland or the United Kingdom.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top. If we start collecting something new, we will add it here first.
